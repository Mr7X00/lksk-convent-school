About Our Institution

A Legacy of Academic Stature & Ethical Character

Established in 2017, L.K.S.K Convent School was founded with a singular conviction: that every child in Panditpur, Sohawal, and the greater Ayodhya district deserves access to exemplary scholastic training, rooted in timeless Indian moral values and modern pedagogical standards.

Our campus integrates disciplined character formation with innovative STEM exploration, expressive arts, and physical athletics. We nurture young minds to emerge as articulate, empathetic, and resilient contributors to society.
