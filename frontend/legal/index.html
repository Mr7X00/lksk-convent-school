Section 1

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: Academic Session 2026–2027 | Last Revised: 2026

L.K.S.K Convent School (“the School”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), located at Panditpur, Sohawal, Ayodhya, Uttar Pradesh — 224188, is firmly committed to safeguarding the personal privacy of our students, prospective parents, guardians, faculty, and website visitors.

1.1 Information Collected Through Official Online Forms

Our website collects information voluntarily submitted by parents, guardians, and visitors through our institutional forms:

  • Admission Inquiry Form: Student’s Full Name, Date of Birth, Gender, Standard/Grade applied for, Father’s Name, Mother’s Name, Primary Contact Telephone Number, WhatsApp Number, Email Address, and Residential Locality/Address.
  • General Contact & Grievance Form: Inquirer Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Subject, and nature of institutional query.

1.2 Purpose and Use of Collected Information

The information collected through this website is strictly utilized for educational and school administrative functions:

  • Processing admission inquiries and scheduling student interaction sessions;
  • Communicating official school schedules, admission confirmations, and fee guidance;
  • Responding to academic, transport, or facility inquiries submitted by guardians;
  • Maintaining institutional records as required by state and regional educational guidelines.

1.3 Data Protection & Third-Party Disclosure Policy

We never sell, rent, commercialize, or trade personal information to third parties, advertising agencies, or marketing networks. Personal information is accessible solely to authorized administrative and admission officers of L.K.S.K Convent School on a strictly need-to-know basis.

1.4 Cookies and Browser Storage

This website employs minimal, non-tracking local session storage only for essential interface preferences, such as remembering dismissal of the top announcement banner or controlling the display frequency of the admission alert modal. We do not employ third-party cross-site advertising trackers.

Section 2

Terms & Conditions of Website Use

Institutional Governance and Digital Access Standards

By accessing or utilizing the official website of L.K.S.K Convent School, visitors and users agree to be bound by the following terms of use:

2.1 Intellectual Property & School Assets

All digital content hosted on this website — including the school crest/logo, campus photographs, academic syllabi, announcements, documents, and code — are the intellectual property of L.K.S.K Convent School or its technical licensors. Reproduction, redistribution, or modification of any school visual asset without prior written institutional consent is strictly prohibited.

2.2 Permissible Use

This website is provided solely for legitimate academic inquiries, guardian communication, student verification, and admission registration. Any attempt to compromise server infrastructure, launch denial-of-service attacks, bypass administrative authentication, or submit malicious or fraudulent data through inquiry forms is unlawful and subject to legal prosecution.

2.3 Online Inquiry Does Not Guarantee Admission

Submission of an online admission inquiry form conveys an expression of interest and does not constitute a formal guarantee of student admission. Formal admission is finalized strictly upon verification of eligibility documents, meeting age criteria, interaction, and compliance with school admission guidelines at the administrative office.

Section 3

Disclaimer & Official Notice

Accuracy, Academic Updates, and Official Channels

While L.K.S.K Convent School endeavors to maintain up-to-date and accurate information regarding academic calendars, fee structures, holiday dates, and school guidelines:

  • Physical Notice Board Precedence: In any unforeseen discrepancy between website content and physical circulars issued directly by the school administrative office, the signed physical notification issued under the seal of the Principal/Manager shall take official precedence.
  • Calendar Adjustments: Holiday schedules, examination timetables, and school operating hours are subject to government administrative notifications (District Magistrate / Education Board directives) and weather contingencies.
  • External Portals: Links to external governmental or educational board portals are provided for informational convenience. L.K.S.K Convent School bears no liability for the availability or privacy practices of external third-party domains.
