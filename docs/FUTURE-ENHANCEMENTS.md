## 1. Roadmap Overview

The primary objective of the initial engineering lifecycle (Phases 1–17) was to construct an accessible, production-ready, search-optimized institutional website, admissions CRM, and administrative CMS.

To prevent scope creep and maintain architectural stability, the following capabilities have been cataloged for future development phases following live operational acceptance.

---

## 2. Planned Future Initiatives

### 2.1 Online Fee Payment Gateway
- **Description:** Direct integration with an RBI-approved payment aggregator (e.g. Razorpay, Cashfree, or PayU).
- **Features:**
  - Online student fee installment payments via UPI, NetBanking, Debit/Credit Cards.
  - Automated digital receipt generation and PDF download for parents.
  - Reconciliation ledger for school accounting.

### 2.2 Parent & Student Portal (LKSK Connect)
- **Description:** Password-protected portal for enrolled student families.
- **Features:**
  - Monthly attendance percentage tracking.
  - Term examination marks and downloadable report cards.
  - Direct circular delivery and teacher-parent communication log.

### 2.3 Automated WhatsApp Business API Integration
- **Description:** Enterprise WhatsApp Business API integration (via Twilio or Meta Graph API).
- **Features:**
  - Automated WhatsApp confirmation dispatched to parents upon admission inquiry submission.
  - Emergency holiday notifications and urgent administrative circular broadcasting.

### 2.4 Transport Fleet GPS Tracking
- **Description:** Real-time school bus/van GPS integration.
- **Features:**
  - Live transport route tracking for parents during morning pickup and afternoon drop-off.
  - Speed monitoring and arrival alerts.

### 2.5 Progressive Web App (PWA) Capabilities
- **Description:** ServiceWorker caching and manifest enhancements.
- **Features:**
  - "Add to Home Screen" app-like installability on Android and iOS devices.
  - Offline access to previously viewed school notices and emergency contacts.
