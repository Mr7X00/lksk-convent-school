## 1. Accessing the Administrator Portal

1. Open your web browser and navigate to:
   ```text
   https://<your-domain>/admin/login
   ```
2. Enter your authorized administrator username (or registered email address) and password.
3. Click **Sign In to Admin Portal**.
4. Upon successful authentication, you will be directed to the unified administrative dashboard at `/admin/`.
5. **Security Notice:** The system automatically locks the account for 15 minutes after 5 consecutive failed login attempts. To end your administrative session securely, always click **Sign Out** in the top navigation bar.

---

## 2. Managing Homepage & Hero Content

### 2.1 Hero Carousel Slides
- Navigate to **Hero Slides** in the sidebar.
- Click **Add New Slide**.
- Provide a clear, descriptive **Title** (e.g. `Holistic Education & Values`), **Subtitle** (e.g. `Admission Open for Session 2026–27`), and select an action button target (e.g. `/academic/admission-inquiry/`).
- Upload high-resolution campus photography (landscape 16:9 aspect ratio recommended).
- Set status to **Active** and assign a display order number (1 to 5).
- Click **Save Slide**.

### 2.2 Urgent Announcements & Top Notification Banner
- Navigate to **Announcements**.
- Click **Create Announcement**.
- Enter the urgent headline (e.g. `Admissions Open for Session 2026–2027 — Register Online`).
- Select category: `Admissions`, `Examinations`, `Holiday`, or `General Notice`.
- Specify an **End Date** so temporary notices automatically disappear after expiration.
- Click **Publish**. The announcement appears instantly across all public website pages.

---

## 3. Photo Gallery & Media Albums

1. Navigate to **Gallery & Media**.
2. Click **Create New Album** (e.g. `Annual Sports Meet 2026`, `Science & Robotics Exhibition`, `Cultural Celebration`).
3. Select an album category: `Campus`, `Events`, `Activities`, `Sports`, or `Celebrations`.
4. Upload photographs (WebP or JPEG format recommended).
5. For every uploaded photo, enter a brief, descriptive **Caption / Alt Text** (e.g. `Students participating in annual 100m sprint race on campus sports field`).
6. Click **Save Album**. The album immediately displays in the public `/gallery/` section.

---

## 4. Faculty & Staff Directory

1. Navigate to **Faculty & Staff**.
2. Click **Add Faculty Member**.
3. Fill in the verified educator details:
   - Full Name (e.g. `Sunita Sharma`)
   - Designation (e.g. `Senior Secondary Teacher`, `Head of Science Department`)
   - Department / Subject Specialization (e.g. `Mathematics`, `English Literature`, `Science`)
   - Educational Qualifications (e.g. `M.Sc., B.Ed.`)
   - Experience in Years (e.g. `8+ Years`)
   - Category (`Teaching Staff`, `Administrative Staff`, `Support Staff`)
   - Professional headshot photograph
4. Click **Publish Staff Record**.

---

## 5. Academic Content & Document Center

### 5.1 Uploading Official PDF Documents & Mandatory Disclosures
1. Navigate to **Documents** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Upload Document**.
3. Select the document classification:
   - `Mandatory Public Disclosure`
   - `Syllabus`
   - `Academic Calendar`
   - `Annual Holiday List`
   - `Fee Structure`
4. Enter the document title (e.g. `Academic Calendar 2026–2027`) and academic session.
5. Upload the PDF file (maximum 10 MB).
6. Click **Save & Publish**. The PDF is instantly linked under the relevant School Corner and `/academic/` pages.

### 5.2 Circulars & Official Notice Board
1. Navigate to **Notices & Circulars**.
2. Click **Create Notice**.
3. Input the circular title, reference number, publication date, and body text.
4. Attach an optional circular PDF if issued under the seal of the Principal.
5. Click **Post Notice**.

---

## 6. Managing Admission Inquiries (CRM Inbox)

1. Navigate to **Admission Inquiries**.
2. The dashboard displays all prospective student submissions received via `/academic/admission-inquiry/`.
3. Click any inquiry row to review full student details:
   - Student Full Name, Date of Birth, Gender, Grade/Standard applied for.
   - Father's Name, Mother's Name, Primary Contact Telephone, Email Address, and Home Address.
4. Update the student application status:
   - `New`: Unprocessed initial inquiry.
   - `In Review`: Academic committee reviewing documents.
   - `Contacted`: School office telephoned guardian for campus interaction.
   - `Admitted`: Admission finalized and fee receipt issued.
   - `Closed`: Inquiry completed or withdrawn.
5. Enter internal administrative notes in the **Admin Notes** field.
6. Click **Export to CSV** to download a clean, spreadsheet-ready report for admission committee meetings.

---

## 7. Website Settings & Contact Information

1. Navigate to **Website Settings**.
2. Update the verified institutional details:
   - **Primary Phone Number:** Update when new school landline or mobile is assigned.
   - **Official WhatsApp Number:** When configured, the floating chat button activates on the website automatically.
   - **Board Affiliation Number & School Code:** Enter official registration numbers to display in public compliance areas.
   - **Google Maps Embed Link:** Paste the official Google Maps iframe URL to render the campus map on the `/contact/` page.
   - **Social Media Links:** Paste verified Facebook, Instagram, or YouTube channel links. (Leaving a field blank hides the icon automatically).
3. Click **Save Website Settings**. Changes reflect immediately across all page footers and contact cards.
